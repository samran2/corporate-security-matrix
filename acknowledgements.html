Acknowledgements

Built for shared resilience.

The Corporate Security Matrix began with a simple ambition: make enterprise security easier to see, discuss and improve across professional boundaries.

Project origin

Idea and direction.

Sami Rantanen is recognized as the originator of the Corporate Security Matrix initiative and its open, globally reusable direction.

ORIGIN

One shared view

The project was conceived to connect physical, people, operational, cyber, integrity, safety and resilience decisions in one practical model.

PURPOSE

Protection through clarity

Its public-purpose intent is to help organizations anticipate material harm, coordinate earlier and recover with less confusion.

OPEN SOURCE

Useful beyond one organization

The work is published under the MIT License so practitioners can inspect, adapt and improve it openly.

Stewardship

How contributions earn trust.

The matrix is an original synthesis. Its quality depends on transparent sources, clear scope, careful language and constructive professional challenge.

01

Authoritative sources first

Prefer official standards bodies, intergovernmental organizations, regulators and public authorities over secondary summaries, while stating each source’s scope and applicability.

02

No borrowed authority

Keep the project’s original synthesis distinct from normative requirements, legal advice and formal certification.

03

Review what matters

Challenge factual accuracy, applicability, unintended harm and whether a recommendation can be evidenced in practice.

Contribute

Help make the model more useful and more defensible.

Report broken sources, unclear scope, factual errors or practical gaps through the public repository. Read the content-review record for this edition. Never include sensitive operational information.
